# Practice brief: Ruang Reka Demo

All identities and projects below are fictional examples for a local preview.
The website must display a demo label so examples cannot be mistaken for real
business claims. Publication as a real company is not authorized.

## Company and goals

- Name: Ruang Reka Demo.
- Industry: interior design studio for retail and office spaces.
- Audience: business owners and office managers who want to understand the services.
- Language: English.
- Practice goal: explain services and the work process through a one-page website.
- Visual direction: warm, tidy, readable text, neutral colors with green accents.

## Services in the demo scenario

1. **Interior design:** develop concepts, layouts, and material choices.
   Deliverables include visual concepts and a layout plan based on the brief.
2. **3D visualization:** help clients see the proposed design before implementation.
   Deliverables include visualizations based on an available design.
3. **Implementation support:** help align construction work with the design
   through coordination and review within an agreed scope.

## Conceptual portfolio examples

- **Small retail concept:** simulate display, checkout, and visitor circulation
  needs. Example role: develop layout concepts and visualizations.
- **Small team office concept:** simulate work, meeting, and storage needs.
  Example role: develop zoning and material choices.

Both must be labeled **conceptual project / demo example**. There are no clients,
completed project photos, transaction values, or numerical results to claim.

## Assets, contacts, and exercise limits

- No logo is available; use the company name as a demo wordmark.
- No original photos are available; visuals may be clearly labeled demo illustrations.
- Email, phone, address, and WhatsApp details are unavailable.
- A contact layout may be built with a note that contact details are not yet provided.
- Form submission, CMS, analytics, hosting, and publication are outside scope.
- Use the existing project stack; for a new project, choose a simple implementation.
- Deliverables: code, local preview, run instructions, and QA results with their limits.
